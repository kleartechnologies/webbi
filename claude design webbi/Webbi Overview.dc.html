Assumptions

  • Malaysia launch: RM pricing, WhatsApp as the default contact channel, FPX / e-wallet / card at checkout.
  • Creator side is mobile-first; everything works one-handed on a phone. Desktop is the same layout, wider.
  • Paywall sits at Publish: anyone can describe a business and see their site free; RM149.90 makes it live at webbi.my/your-name.
  • Account is created only when needed (to save or publish), with Google or phone OTP.
  • Copy is mixed EN + BM, the way small-business Malaysians actually write.
  • Photos are labelled placeholders; real photography drops in later.

Design reasoning

  • One question first. Onboarding opens with a single big box, “What do you do?”. Webbi pre-fills the rest; the user only confirms.
  • Generation is a moment, not a spinner. Five honest steps, then a full-bleed reveal of the finished site with one line: “Your Webbi is ready.”
  • Editor = structured forms, not a canvas. Five tabs (Business, Products & Services, Photos, Design, Settings) with the live site pinned above.
  • Public sites share bones, not skin. Same section grammar (hero → quick nav → sections → sticky CTA); type, palette and CTA verb change per business.
  • The CTA is the product. Every site ends in one bottom-pinned action the customer understands: order, book, quote, test drive, enquire.

Out of scope for V1 (by brief)

CRM, analytics, email marketing, SEO tools, appointment management, inventory, POS, full e-commerce, automation, teams, custom domains, conversational AI editing. The editor's Settings tab leaves room for custom domains later without redesign.
